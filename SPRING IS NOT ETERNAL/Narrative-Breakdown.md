# Spring is not eternal
## Genre: 
Japanese parable
## Main theme: 
The story follows a gōshi samurai who blindly believed in the righteousness of his daimyō. Only after facing the consequences of his own actions did he realize that he had spent his entire life believing in a lie.
## Idea: 
The idea of the story is to convey the importance of morality, critical thinking, and considering the consequences of one's actions.The story also explores the themes of redemption and the equality of people, both morally and physically.Finally, the story was created to introduce the reader to Japanese traditions, culture, and worldview.
## Key characters: 
* The Gōshi Samurai
* The Daimyō
* The Mother of the Executed Man
* The Rice Thief
 ## Alternative Branches

The interactive version of the story includes several key choices that allow the player to influence the protagonist's relationship with his daimyō, his actions, and his eventual fate.

### 1. Accept or Refuse the Assignment

At the beginning of the story, the gōshi samurai receives a direct order from his daimyō to eliminate Nakanū Nishimura.

- **Accept the assignment** — The samurai follows the order and begins his journey to Izumo.
- **Refuse the assignment** — The samurai is immediately executed.

**Narrative purpose:** This choice establishes an important rule of the world: a samurai has no right to refuse a direct order from his daimyō.

---

### 2. Accept or Refuse the Villagers' Help

During his journey, the samurai reaches a village where the locals offer him food, shelter, and assistance in crossing the river.

- **Accept the villagers' help** — The samurai stays in the village, but his decision worsens his relationship with the daimyō because he delays the mission.
- **Refuse the offer** — The samurai rejects their help and continues his journey, maintaining his relationship with the daimyō.

**Narrative purpose:** This choice creates a conflict between human compassion and unquestioning loyalty to the daimyō.

---

### 3. Kill or Spare the Rice Thief

When the samurai finally finds the man he was ordered to eliminate, he discovers that he does not appear to be the dangerous criminal he expected.

- **Kill the rice thief** — The samurai follows the order, leading to the original events of the story and his eventual realization of the consequences of his actions.
- **Spare the rice thief** — The samurai refuses to carry out the execution, ultimately provoking the daimyō's anger.

**Narrative purpose:** This is the central moral choice of the story: whether to blindly follow an order or question the morality behind it.

---

### 4. Return to the Daimyō or Remain in Exile

After realizing the truth about the system he served, the samurai has one final choice.

- **Return to the daimyō** — The samurai confronts his daimyō, leading to the original ending.
- **Remain in exile** — The samurai refuses to return and lives in exile under the constant threat of being hunted by the daimyō.

---

### 5. The Final Fate

After returning to the daimyō, the protagonist faces the final consequences of his choices.

- **Perform Harakiri** — The samurai takes his own life through harakiri, choosing death rather than continuing to serve the corrupted system.

- **Face Execution** — If the protagonist failed to complete the original mission, the daimyō executes him for disobeying or failing to fulfill his order.

- **Accept the Reward** — The samurai accepts the rice offered as his reward and returns home, choosing to continue his life despite everything that happened.

## Quest Design

### Quest: The Daimyō's Order

**Initial Objective:**  
Eliminate the rice thief Nakanū Nishimura in Izumo.

— After the opening cutscene, the protagonist receives a direct order from his daimyō to eliminate the target.

### 1. Prepare for the Mission

**Objective:** Obtain the samurai's armor and weapon.

— The protagonist travels to the storage building to retrieve his equipment.

**Cutscene:** Ritual purification.

— Before entering the shūri, the samurai performs a purification ritual at the temizuya.

**Mini-Quest:** Prepare the equipment.

— The player explores the shūri and retrieves the protagonist's armor and katana.

---

### 2. Travel to Izumo

**Objective:** Reach Izumo.

— The protagonist begins his journey through the countryside.

— During the journey, the player encounters a village whose inhabitants offer food, shelter, and assistance.

**Choice:**
- Accept the villagers' help.
- Refuse their offer and continue the journey.

— The choice affects the protagonist's relationship with the daimyō.

— If the protagonist accepts the villagers' hospitality, he remains in the village and avoids the later fall into the river.

— If he refuses, he continues along the original route and eventually falls into the river.

— The river sequence is primarily a visual consequence of the player's earlier choice and does not significantly alter the main quest.

---

### 3. Eliminate the Target

**Objective:** Find and eliminate Nakanū Nishimura.

— The protagonist reaches Izumo and encounters the target working in the rice fields.

— The player must decide whether to follow the daimyō's order or spare the rice thief.

---

### 4. Find Shelter

— After the confrontation with the target, rain begins to fall.

**Mini-Quest:** Find shelter.

— The protagonist must find a nearby house where he can take shelter from the rain.

— This leads to the encounter with the mother of the executed man and the protagonist's realization of what he has done.

---

### 5. Decide the Protagonist's Fate

— After discovering the truth, the protagonist must decide what to do next.

**Choice:**
- Remain in exile and live as a hermit.
- Return to the daimyō.

#### Exile Ending

— The protagonist refuses to return to the daimyō and lives in exile under the constant threat of being hunted.

**Ending.**

#### Return to the Daimyō

— The protagonist returns to the daimyō and confronts him.

— A new objective is introduced:

**Objective:** Confront the daimyō.

— The confrontation leads to several possible endings.

---

### 6. Final Outcomes

#### Harakiri

— The protagonist chooses to take his own life through harakiri.

**Ending.**

#### Execution

— If the protagonist failed to complete the original objective, the daimyō executes him.

**Ending.**

#### Reward

— The protagonist accepts the daimyō's reward of rice and returns home.

**Ending.**
