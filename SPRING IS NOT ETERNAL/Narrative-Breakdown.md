# Spring is not eternal
## Genre: 
Japanese parable
## Main theme: 
The story follows a gōshi samurai who blindly believed in the righteousness of his daimyō. Only after facing the consequences of his own actions did he realize that he had spent his entire life believing in a lie.
## Idea: 
The idea of the story is to convey the importance of morality, critical thinking, and considering the consequences of one's actions.The story also explores the themes of redemption and the equality of people, both morally and physically.Finally, the story was created to introduce the reader to Japanese traditions, culture, and worldview.
## Key characters: 
* The Gōshi Samurai
* The Daimyō
* The Mother of the Executed Man
